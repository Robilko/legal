1. General

This Policy explains what data the BasketSnap mobile app for Android (package ru.robilko.basket_snap, the “App”) processes, why, who receives it and how long it is kept. The App shows schedules, results and statistics of basketball leagues, teams and players.

Personal data operator and developer of the App:

Aleksandr Aleksandrovich Robilko, self-employed (professional income tax payer), Moscow, Russian Federation.
Email: sasha.robson@yandex.ru

This Policy applies to the App from Google Play, RuStore and Huawei AppGallery. The App needs no sign-up and has no accounts. By using the App, you confirm that you have read this Policy; if you do not agree with it, uninstall the App.

2. What data is processed

2.1. Data you provide yourself

  • Email to the developer. “Email the developer” opens your email app with the address and subject filled in; you send the email yourself. The developer receives your address, your message and any attachments you add.
  • Error report. On an error screen, “Send report” adds to the email the error details, the device manufacturer and model, the Android version and the App version. You see this text and can edit it before sending.
  • Telegram chat. Messages in the BasketSnap chat are processed by Telegram under its own rules.

2.2. Data sent automatically

The App does not link this data to your identity and does not send it to the developer.

API-Basketball requests

What
Your device's IP address; request parameters — league, team, season, player or game; the device time zone (for the list of games on the selected day in Match Center); search text.
Search
A search is sent only when you tap “Search” on the keyboard. The App first looks up Russian text in its names directory and, if nothing is found, transliterates it: the API receives the Latin version.
Recipient
API-SPORTS — the API-Basketball service (v1.basketball.api-sports.io).
Purpose
To get schedules, results, rosters and statistics.

Images: logos and flags

What
Your device's IP address and the usual details of a web request, such as the User-Agent.
Recipient
The CDN service of CDN-video LLC (CDNvideo), which delivers API-Basketball logos and flags and some logos from the names directory. Other directory logos come from league and club websites and other sources (for example, infobasket.su, russiabasket.ru, baskethotel.com, web.archive.org).
Purpose
To show team and league logos and country flags, including in game reminders.

Firebase (Google)

What
The Firebase installation ID — random, created on first launch and not linked to your identity; device language and region; time zone; Android and App versions; package name; IP address.
Recipient
Google LLC — Firebase Installations, Remote Config and Cloud Firestore.
Purpose
Remote Config provides the App's settings — for example, that a new version is out. From Cloud Firestore the App reads a directory of Russian names and logos of leagues and teams. The App never writes to Firestore, and search queries are not sent there.

Crash reports (Tracer)

What
On a crash or freeze — a technical report: where in the code the error occurred, device model, Android and App versions, network type. On running out of memory — a memory snapshot of the App, which Tracer, according to its documentation, clears of content before sending. The size of the App's folders if it exceeds a threshold. Statistics of crash-free sessions. Details of non-critical network errors — without request addresses or search text. Tracer keeps its own random installation ID in the App.
Recipient
VK LLC — the Tracer service. Data is stored in Russia.
Purpose
To find and fix errors in the App.

2.3. Data kept only on your device

  • Settings: theme, “Hide today's game scores”, Match Center filters.
  • Favourite leagues and teams — for the “Favourites” filter.
  • Game reminders: game, teams, league and logo addresses.
  • Cache: API responses (up to 100 entries, from 5 minutes to a day; they contain search text), logos and flags (up to 250 MB), the countries directory, a copy of the names directory.
  • Firebase and Tracer installation IDs.

This data is not sent to the developer. If Android backup is turned on, the system may save the settings, favourites, directories and installation IDs to your Google account; the API response cache is excluded from the backup. You manage the backup in Android settings; the developer has no access to it.

2.4. What the App does not collect

  • Location.
  • Contacts, photos, files, camera or microphone access.
  • Advertising ID — there are no ads in the App.
  • Analytics: which screens you open and what you tap.
  • Payment data — there are no payments in the App; support for the project goes through the YooMoney website.
  • Sign-in data — there is no sign-up.

6. Retention and deletion

  • On your device — while the App is installed. “Clear cache” in Settings deletes stored API responses and images. All App data is deleted together with the App or in Android settings: “Apps → BasketSnap → Storage → Clear storage”. Tap the star to remove a favourite and the bell again to remove a reminder.
  • Firebase — retention is set by Google, see Privacy and Security in Firebase.
  • Tracer — crash events are kept for 90 days, according to the Tracer documentation.
  • API-SPORTS, CDNvideo and logo websites — under their own rules.
  • Correspondence with the developer is kept in the mailbox with no time limit. The developer will delete it at your request.
  • Android backup is kept in your Google account and managed by you.

7. Your rights

Under Federal Law No. 152‑FZ “On Personal Data”, you have the right to:

  • get information about what data of yours is processed;
  • ask to correct, block or delete data that is incomplete, outdated, inaccurate or processed unlawfully;
  • withdraw your consent — for example, ask to delete your correspondence;
  • appeal against the operator's actions to Roskomnadzor (rkn.gov.ru, in Russian) or in court.

Send requests to sasha.robson@yandex.ru. Response time: 10 working days.

The technical data received by the services in section 4 is not linked to your identity, so the developer cannot find it by your request. You can delete the data on your device yourself — see section 6.

8. Children

The App is not designed specifically for children and does not take part in the Google Play Families program. Age ratings: 3+ in Google Play and Huawei AppGallery, 0+ in RuStore.

The App does not knowingly collect personal data from children: there is no sign-up, and the technical data received by the services is not linked to anyone's identity. If a child under 14 has written to the developer without parental consent, a parent or legal guardian can ask to delete the correspondence at sasha.robson@yandex.ru.

9. Security

  • The App contacts API-Basketball, Firebase and Tracer over HTTPS; images are loaded over HTTPS too — Android does not open unencrypted addresses.
  • Data on your device is kept in the App's folder, which other apps cannot access.
  • Logs with request contents are written only in debug builds, never in published versions.
  • No method of transmitting or storing data is completely secure.

10. Notifications

The App only shows game reminders you set yourself with the bell — 10 minutes before the start. Reminders work on your device without a server; there are no advertising or push campaigns. On Android 13 and later, the App asks for notification permission when you set your first reminder.

You can turn notifications off by tapping the bell again or in Android settings: “Apps → BasketSnap → Notifications”.
