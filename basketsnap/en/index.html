In short

  • BasketSnap has no sign-up, accounts, ads, analytics or payments. The App does not request your location.
  • To show games, the App calls API-Basketball and downloads logos. These services see your device's IP address, and API-Basketball also sees your search text. In Russia, API-Basketball requests go through the developer's server. API-Basketball then sees the server's address instead of your device's; the server itself stores neither IP addresses nor request contents.
  • The widget and game notifications work on your device, with no notification server. To stay current, the App itself, without being opened, asks API-Basketball for the day's games: for the widget once a day at night and when you tap “Refresh”, for notifications only for teams whose bell you have turned on.
  • Settings, favourites, reminders and quiz results stay on your device.
  • Firebase (Google) provides the App's settings and a names directory, and Tracer (VK) receives crash reports. For this, they get technical data about the device and the App.
  • You send emails to the developer yourself — they contain your address and what you wrote.
  • Questions and requests: sasha.robson@yandex.ru.

1. General

This Policy explains what data the BasketSnap mobile app for Android (package ru.robilko.basket_snap, the “App”) processes, why, who receives it and how long it is kept. The App shows schedules, results and statistics of basketball leagues, teams and players.

Personal data operator and developer of the App:

Aleksandr Aleksandrovich Robilko, self-employed (professional income tax payer), Moscow, Russian Federation.
Email: sasha.robson@yandex.ru

This Policy applies to the App from Google Play, RuStore and Huawei AppGallery. The App needs no sign-up and has no accounts. By using the App, you confirm that you have read this Policy; if you do not agree with it, uninstall the App.

2. What data is processed

2.1. Data you provide yourself

  • Email to the developer. “Email the developer” opens your email app with the address and subject filled in; you send the email yourself. The developer receives your address, your message and any attachments you add.
  • Error report. On an error screen, “Send report” adds to the email the error details, the device manufacturer and model, the Android version and the App version. You see this text and can edit it before sending.
  • Telegram chat. Messages in the BasketSnap chat are processed by Telegram under its own rules.

2.2. Data sent automatically

The App does not link this data to your identity. The developer receives it only when API-Basketball requests go through the developer's server, which stores neither IP addresses nor request parameters.

API-Basketball requests

What
Your device's IP address; request parameters — league, team, season, player or game; the device time zone (for the list of games on the selected day in Match Center, for updating live games on a league screen and for the widget and notifications); search text.
Search
A search is sent only when you tap “Search” on the keyboard. The App first looks up Russian text in its names directory and, if nothing is found, transliterates it: the API receives the Latin version.
Recipient
API-SPORTS — the API-Basketball service (v1.basketball.api-sports.io).
Route
Directly or through the developer's server (see below). The route depends on the country of the mobile network, or on the time zone if there is no SIM card: in Russia requests go through the server, in other countries — directly. If one route does not respond, the App tries the other. The App determines the country on the device and does not send it anywhere; the developer can change the order of routes in the App's remote settings.
Purpose
To get schedules, results, rosters and statistics.

Requests without opening the App: widget and notifications

What
The same API-Basketball requests as described above: your device's IP address and time zone. A request carries only the date and the time zone — the day's games in a single request for all favourites; your favourite teams and leagues are not sent, the App picks them out on the device.
When
The widget — once a day at night, when the App starts if the widget's data is not from today, and when you tap “Refresh” in the widget. Notifications — only if a favourite team's bell is on: the schedule for today and tomorrow every 12 hours and, after a game starts, its result until the final score arrives. The number of such requests per day is limited.
Purpose
To show the day's games in the widget and to send a start reminder and the final score without a notification server.
Turning off
Remove the widget from the home screen; turn off a team's bell or the switches in the App's settings (“Notifications”).

The developer's server

What
API-Basketball requests that go through the server: your device's IP address and the request parameters listed above.
Where
The developer's server api.basket-snap.ru in a data centre of Selectel JSC, Russia.
Purpose
To load data where the direct connection to API-Basketball works poorly, for example in Russia. The server forwards the request to API-SPORTS and returns the response to the App.
Retention
IP addresses and request parameters are not stored. The server log — time, API section, response code, size and time — is kept for 7 days.

Images: logos and flags

What
Your device's IP address and the usual details of a web request, such as the User-Agent.
Recipient
The CDN service of CDN-video LLC (CDNvideo), which delivers API-Basketball logos and flags and some logos from the names directory. Other directory logos come from league and club websites and other sources (for example, infobasket.su, russiabasket.ru, baskethotel.com, web.archive.org).
Purpose
To show team and league logos and country flags, including in game reminders.

Firebase (Google)

What
The Firebase installation ID — random, created on first launch and not linked to your identity; device language and region; time zone; Android and App versions; package name; IP address.
Recipient
Google LLC — Firebase Installations, Remote Config and Cloud Firestore.
Purpose
Remote Config provides the App's settings — for example, that a new version is out. From Cloud Firestore the App reads a directory of Russian names and logos of leagues and teams. The App never writes to Firestore, and search queries are not sent there.

Crash reports (Tracer)

What
On a crash or freeze — a technical report: where in the code the error occurred, device model, Android and App versions, network type. On running out of memory — a memory snapshot of the App, which Tracer, according to its documentation, clears of content before sending. The size of the App's folders if it exceeds a threshold. Statistics of crash-free sessions. Details of non-critical network errors — without request addresses or search text. Tracer keeps its own random installation ID in the App.
Recipient
VK LLC — the Tracer service. Data is stored in Russia.
Purpose
To find and fix errors in the App.

2.3. Data kept only on your device

  • Settings: theme, “Hide today's game scores”, Match Center filters.
  • Favourite leagues and teams — for the “Favourites” filter.
  • Game reminders: game, teams, league and logo addresses.
  • Team notifications: which favourite teams have the bell turned on, the schedule of upcoming notifications and which ones have already been shown.
  • Widget: a snapshot of the day's games — all games of the day from the API response, with your favourites picked out when it is shown: time, teams, league, score, logo addresses — and the time of the last update.
  • Quiz: personal statistics (record, number of rounds played, answers and correct answers, best streak), the history of the last 20 rounds — league, season, chosen team and player, level, result and finish time — and the last “Custom round” choice: the league with its logo address, season and level.
  • Service data: a counter of background requests for the day, which limits usage.
  • Cache: API responses (up to 100 entries, from 5 minutes to a day; they contain search text), logos and flags (up to 250 MB), the countries directory, a copy of the names directory.
  • Firebase and Tracer installation IDs.

This data is not sent to the developer. If Android backup is turned on, the system may save the settings, favourites, quiz data, directories and installation IDs to your Google account; the API response cache, the widget snapshot and the data of pending notifications are excluded from the backup. You manage the backup in Android settings; the developer has no access to it.

2.4. What the App does not collect

  • Location.
  • Contacts, photos, files, camera or microphone access.
  • Advertising ID — there are no ads in the App.
  • Analytics: which screens you open and what you tap.
  • Payment data — there are no payments in the App; support for the project goes through the YooMoney website.
  • Sign-in data — there is no sign-up.

6. Retention and deletion

  • On your device — while the App is installed. “Clear cache” in Settings deletes stored API responses and images. All App data is deleted together with the App or in Android settings: “Apps → BasketSnap → Storage → Clear storage”. Tap the star to remove a favourite and the bell again to remove a reminder or a team's notifications; remove the widget from the home screen to stop its nightly requests.
  • Firebase — retention is set by Google, see Privacy and Security in Firebase.
  • Tracer — crash events are kept for 90 days, according to the Tracer documentation.
  • The developer's server does not store IP addresses or request parameters; its log without them is kept for 7 days.
  • API-SPORTS, Selectel, CDNvideo and logo websites — under their own rules.
  • Correspondence with the developer is kept in the mailbox with no time limit. The developer will delete it at your request.
  • Android backup is kept in your Google account and managed by you.

8. Children

The App is not designed specifically for children and does not take part in the Google Play Families program. Age ratings: 3+ in Google Play and Huawei AppGallery, 0+ in RuStore.

The App does not knowingly collect personal data from children: there is no sign-up, and the technical data received by the services is not linked to anyone's identity. If a child under 14 has written to the developer without parental consent, a parent or legal guardian can ask to delete the correspondence at sasha.robson@yandex.ru.

9. Security

  • The App contacts API-Basketball (directly and through the developer's server), Firebase and Tracer over HTTPS; images are loaded over HTTPS too — Android does not open unencrypted addresses.
  • Data on your device is kept in the App's folder, which other apps cannot access.
  • Logs with request contents are written only in debug builds, never in published versions.
  • No method of transmitting or storing data is completely secure.

10. Notifications

The App only shows notifications about games that you turned on yourself with the bell; there are no advertising or push campaigns:

  • Reminder — 10 minutes before a game starts: the bell on a game or on a favourite team.
  • Final score — a few minutes after a game ends, only for favourite teams with the bell on. If “Hide today's game scores” is on in Settings, the notification does not show the score.

Notifications work on your device; there is no notification server. To know when a team's games start and how they end, the App itself, without being opened, asks API-Basketball for the schedule for today and tomorrow (every 12 hours) and, after a game starts, for its result (see section 2.2). On Android 13 and later, the App asks for notification permission when you first turn on a bell.

You can turn notifications off by tapping the bell again, with the switches in the App's settings (“Notifications”) or in Android settings: “Apps → BasketSnap → Notifications”.
