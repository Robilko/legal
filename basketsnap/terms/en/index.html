6. Quiz and predictions

The “Arena” tab is a game played for fun: a quiz on past games and predictions of upcoming games (who will win and by how many points). Correct answers and predictions earn virtual points, and points earn titles.

  • No money. Participation is free; there are no stakes, entry fees, prizes or winnings. Points and titles have no monetary value, are not property, currency or bonuses, and cannot be exchanged, transferred or redeemed. This is not gambling or a lottery.
  • The rules are in the App. How many points a correct outcome, an exact margin and a streak earn, and when a prediction is void (game cancelled or postponed, forfeit or technical result, tie in regulation, no result in time), is shown on the prediction screen and on the “Arena” tab. The Developer may change the rules and titles in new versions of the App.
  • Results come from API-Basketball. A prediction is settled against the game result received from the data source (section 4) when the App is open or refreshing data. An error or delay at the source may affect the settlement; settled predictions are not reviewed manually.
  • Stored on your device. Points, history and titles are kept on your device and in your backup (section 9). Uninstalling the App without a backup deletes them for good; they move to another device only via a backup.
  • No compensation. Because points and titles have no monetary value, no compensation is provided for their loss, change or recalculation. The Developer fixes a scoring error, once found, in a new version of the App.
